# Major Projects & Impact (Draft Summary)

This year I supported several key treasury and financial systems, including BSDRate, Treasury Posting Interface (TPI), OPI cross-application services, Fedline Advantage, and Aladdin. Except for Treasury Rate Tool 765, all of these systems were new to me. I spent the year learning how each system worked while still delivering steady results and closing out a large volume of JIRA stories. I ramped up in an environment with no documentation and fully custom code, using about 20% team support, 20% business partner support, and 60% self-directed learning.

My largest impact area was BSDRate, where I completed the majority of my work. I helped maintain and enhance several jobs and processes that support daily rate updates and downstream reporting. I also delivered updates across TPI and other shared systems, helping keep data moving reliably between platforms. Across all systems, I focused on fixing defects, improving controls, supporting production stability, and completing enhancements that kept our processes running smoothly.

I handled both planned and unplanned work, including production support when issues came up. I made sure I understood the root cause of problems and completed fixes that reduced repeat issues. As I learned each system, I also looked for ways to improve workflows, document findings, and reduce confusion for future work.

Through this combination of learning, delivery, and cross-team support, I strengthened my knowledge of our treasury platforms and contributed to more stable and reliable processes across the applications I worked on.
