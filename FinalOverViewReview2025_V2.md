# Final Overall Performance Review 2025

## Full Overall Performance Summary

This year I supported several important treasury and financial systems, including BSDRate, TPI, OPI cross-application services, Fedline Advantage, and Aladdin. Except for Treasury Rate Tool, all of these systems were new to me. I had to learn how each one worked while still completing my assigned tasks. I learned by working with my team (20%), working with my business partner (20%), and doing most of the learning on my own (60%). Even with these challenges, I delivered steady results throughout the year.

My largest area of work was BSDRate. I updated both the UI and backend to support new curves and calculations, and I made sure the system handled the new data correctly. I also updated several SSIS packages so they would work with the new or changed data being processed. Along with this work, I helped maintain and improve daily rate processes and supported production issues to keep the system stable and reliable.

I adapted quickly to new priorities and a new team. I learned the team’s JIRA setup, sprint routines, and Scrum process while taking on a wide range of tasks. I worked closely with my teammates and business partners to confirm requirements and make sure my work met expectations. I shared what I learned, asked questions when needed, and communicated clearly to keep our work on track.

I stayed focused on work that supported our internal customers. My goal was to reduce system issues, improve accuracy, and help maintain stable daily operations. I followed risk and control steps, tested my work carefully, and raised concerns when something did not look right. I worked with honesty, respect, and care for the data we handle.

I also worked to support an inclusive and positive team environment. I treated others with respect, welcomed different opinions, and shared knowledge to help others understand the systems better.

## Closing Statement

Overall, this was a year of learning, growth, and steady delivery. I took on many new systems, built strong working relationships, and supported key processes that our teams depend on every day. I am more confident in these systems now and look forward to continuing to improve, learn, and provide even stronger support next year.
